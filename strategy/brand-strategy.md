# Los Iconos de la Bachata - Brand Strategy
## Who We Are
Los Iconos de la Bachata is the official merch and cultural platform for Aventura and the legends of bachata. This isn't just a store - it's a home base for people who grew up with this music, who danced to it at quinceañeras and weddings, who still hit repeat on "Obsesión" at 2am.
## Brand Voice
Direct. Proud. Cultural. We speak to the audience like they're already in on it - because they are. Spanish and English, sometimes in the same sentence. Emotion over hype.
