# Campaign: LOS ICONOS DE LA COMUNIDAD

Objective: Build community, drive UGC, grow organic reach
Timeline: July 2026 (ongoing weekly series)
Platforms: All 6

## Concept
Every week we feature a fan who reps the culture. They become an Icono de la Comunidad.
UGC + fan love = organic reach + loyalty.

## Instagram
Caption: 'This week's Icono de la Comunidad is @[handle]. Reppin the culture desde [city]. Tag us in your fit to be featured next. #LosIconosDeLaBachata #IconosDeLaComunidad'

## TikTok
Hook: 'We found the real Los Iconos de la Bachata in the comments'
Show fan photo/video wearing merch. Big energy reaction.

## Twitter/X
'The culture doesn't belong to us. It belongs to all of you. This week's Icono: @[handle]'

## Facebook
'Meet [Name] from [City] - a real Icono de la Bachata. Drop a heart if you rep the culture too.'
